# 00 · Award → Alumni (the umbrella arc)

## What it is

The whole story the platform tells, in ten dots:

**Research award → Opportunity posted → Applicant applies → Offer holder → Registered student → Progressing → Thesis submitted → Viva & corrections → Award (degree) → Alumni.**

This runbook is **not a click-tour of pre-loaded pages** — it's a live end-to-end where we make a real award, publish it, take a real application against it, register the student, watch them through their milestones, submit a thesis, examine it, and graduate them. Every file in this folder is one segment of that live demo, with the exact fields to type and the exact result to look for.

**Terminology heads-up.** "Award" means two things — the *research award* at the start of the arc (a funder gives the institution money) and the *degree award* at the end (the student graduates). Both are covered by their own workflow doc.

## The clicks

**Step 01 · Frame the run.**
- **WHERE** — Sidebar → **Dashboard**.
- **DO** — Say what you're about to do out loud: create an award, use it to publish an opportunity, take an application, register the student, follow them to graduation.
- **SEE** — The dashboard KPI row. Don't linger.
- **SAY** — *"For the next twenty minutes I'm going to build a student from scratch — from the funder's grant to their graduation. Everything I do is real; nothing is pre-loaded."*

**Step 02 · Point at the arc.**
- **WHERE** — Sidebar → **Students** → open any existing student (e.g. Marcus Bell).
- **DO** — Point at the **Journey tracker** under the name.
- **SEE** — Ten numbered stages, current one highlighted.
- **SAY** — *"This is what we're going to walk. By the end of the demo, our new student will be one dot from the right."*

## The line

We're not going to walk pages. We're going to build a student, live, from a funder's grant to their graduation.
