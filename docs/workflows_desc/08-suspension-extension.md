# 08 · Suspension & extension — request one, approve it, watch everything downstream move

## What it is

Priya needs three months off. In this chapter we request a suspension against her record, approve it, and watch three things move automatically: her expected end date shifts by exactly three months, her stipend schedule pauses for the same window, and her workflow tasks pause with her. If she has an employment record (a TA post) it's untouched — the two lifecycles are independent.

## The clicks

**Step 01 · Note the "before" state.**
- **WHERE** — Priya's student page → **Record** section.
- **DO** — Point at **Expected end** (e.g. `2030-10-15`) and the **status** pill (green "Registered").
- **SEE** — One date, one green pill.
- **SAY** — *"Watch these two things. In two clicks they'll both move — and they'll move together."*

**Step 02 · Open the suspension request form.**
- **WHERE** — Same page → **Lifecycle** panel → **Request suspension** button.
- **DO** — Click. Form opens.
- **SEE** — Fields: reason, duration_months, start_date, notes.
- **SAY** — *"A suspension isn't a status change — it's a dated fact with a reason and a duration."*

**Step 03 · Fill the request.**
- **WHERE** — In the form.
- **DO** — Fill: reason `Medical leave`, duration `3`, start_date today, notes `Approved by wellbeing team, doc on file`.
- **SEE** — Preview shows the projected shifted end date.
- **SAY** — *"Preview shows the shift before we commit. No surprises."*

**Step 04 · Save. Approve in the queue.**
- **WHERE** — **Submit request**. Then Sidebar → **Tasks** or **Workflow queue** → find the approval task.
- **DO** — Open it, click **Approve**.
- **SEE** — Status pill on Priya's record flips from green "Registered" to amber "Suspended". Task closes. New audit rows.
- **SAY** — *"Suspension approved. Every downstream field now knows about it."*

**Step 05 · Confirm the "after" state.**
- **WHERE** — Back on Priya's page.
- **DO** — Point at **Expected end** and the **status** pill.
- **SEE** — Expected end shifted forward by three months (e.g. `2031-01-15`). Original date visible in the audit history.
- **SAY** — *"Expected end shifted by exactly three months. Automatically. No admin retyped anything."*

**Step 06 · Show the paused stipend.**
- **WHERE** — Priya's funding arrangement → stipend schedule.
- **DO** — Point at rows in the suspension window (marked "Paused") and the schedule tail extending three months later.
- **SEE** — Three amber "Paused" rows in the middle; the schedule continues three months longer at the end.
- **SAY** — *"Stipend pauses. It doesn't stop — it picks up where we left off, three months later."*

**Step 07 · Show untouched employment.**
- **WHERE** — Priya's person page → **Employment** card (if she has one).
- **DO** — Point at status: still active.
- **SEE** — Two lifecycles side by side: PhD paused, employment running.
- **SAY** — *"Employed students who pause their PhD don't lose their employee record. Two lifecycles, linked but independent."*

## The line

Pausing a PhD is one action, and everything downstream — deadlines, tasks, funding, workflows — moves in step.
