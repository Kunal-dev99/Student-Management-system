# 02 · Opportunities — publish one from the award

## What it is

The award we just created pays for ten studentships. Each becomes an **opportunity** — a real posting with a title, a supervisor, a research area, an eligibility rule, an open/close window, and a link back to the award that funds it. In this chapter we create one, fill in a supervisor, publish it, and land on the page an applicant would see. Opportunities are the seam between money and people.

## The clicks

**Step 01 · Open the opportunities list.**
- **WHERE** — Sidebar → **Recruitment** (megaphone icon) → **Opportunities** tab (or top-of-page toolbar).
- **DO** — Land on the list.
- **SEE** — Existing opportunities as rows: title, supervisor, close date, application count, status pill.
- **SAY** — *"Every studentship we're recruiting for is here. I'm going to add a new one against the award I just created."*

**Step 02 · Start a new opportunity.**
- **WHERE** — Top-right → **+ New opportunity** button.
- **DO** — Click. Form drawer opens.
- **SEE** — Fields: funded_by (award link), title, description, supervisor, research_area, eligibility, open_date, close_date, headcount, remote/on-site.
- **SAY** — *"Every opportunity has to link back to the award that funds it — no unfunded postings by accident."*

**Step 03 · Link it to our award.**
- **WHERE** — First field: **Funded by**.
- **DO** — Type or pick `EP/Z000000/1 · CDT in Applied AI · Cohort 2027`. Autocomplete filters as you type.
- **SEE** — A chip appears with the award reference. Studentship remaining count updates: **9 remaining** (was 10).
- **SAY** — *"Ten studentships on the grant, one now allocated. Nine left. The system won't let me over-commit."*

**Step 04 · Fill the posting.**
- **WHERE** — Rest of the form.
- **DO** — Type:
  - **Title** — `PhD in Explainable Reinforcement Learning`
  - **Supervisor** — pick `Dr. Elena Ford` (existing supervisor record)
  - **Research area** — `Machine Learning` (inherited from award, editable)
  - **Eligibility** — `UK / EU / International. First-class or 2:1 honours in CS, Maths, or Physics. English test if not native.`
  - **Open date** — today
  - **Close date** — six weeks out
  - **Headcount** — `1`
  - **Mode** — `On-site`
- **SEE** — Every required field ticks green.
- **SAY** — *"The supervisor is a real person — the platform checked they're active and have headroom against their cap."*

**Step 05 · Save as draft, then publish.**
- **WHERE** — Bottom of the drawer.
- **DO** — Click **Save as draft** first. Row appears with grey "Draft" pill. Then reopen and click **Publish**.
- **SEE** — Status pill flips to green "Open". A public URL appears in the detail panel.
- **SAY** — *"Draft while I'm iterating — nothing goes public. Publish and it's live for applicants."*

**Step 06 · See the applicant view.**
- **WHERE** — In the detail panel → **View public page** link.
- **DO** — Click. A new tab opens with what an applicant would see.
- **SEE** — Title, supervisor, eligibility, apply button.
- **SAY** — *"That's what an applicant sees. Same record — no separate content site to maintain."*

## The line

The award funded it, the platform posted it. The moment we publish, the outside world enters the arc.
