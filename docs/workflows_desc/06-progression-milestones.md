# 06 · Progression — create a milestone, submit, form the panel, decide

## What it is

Priya's programme has typed milestones — confirmation review (month 9), transfer viva (month 18), annual review (yearly). In this chapter we create her upcoming confirmation review, watch her submit into it, form the review panel, record decisions from each panellist, and land on the final panel outcome. Every step is preserved on one record; if the outcome is appealed later, the appeal opens without overwriting anything.

## The clicks

**Step 01 · Open Priya's milestones panel.**
- **WHERE** — Sidebar → **Students** → Priya Nair → **Milestones** panel.
- **DO** — See the auto-created milestones for her programme.
- **SEE** — Confirmation Review (due month 9), Transfer Viva (month 18), Annual Review 1 (month 12).
- **SAY** — *"Programme-level definitions auto-create the milestones when the student registers. No admin has to know which ones apply."*

**Step 02 · Open the Confirmation Review.**
- **WHERE** — Click the "Confirmation Review" row.
- **DO** — Land on the milestone detail page.
- **SEE** — Three empty panels: **Panel formation**, **Student submission**, **Supervisor assessment**. Status: "Not started".
- **SAY** — *"The milestone is a mini-workflow with three panels. All three feed one decision."*

**Step 03 · Form the panel.**
- **WHERE** — **Panel formation** panel → **+ Add member**.
- **DO** — Add chair `Prof. Sarah Wu`, member `Dr. Rajesh Kumar`. Save.
- **SEE** — Panel row now shows chair + one member. Status of the milestone flips to "Due".
- **SAY** — *"Chair plus at least one member. The platform enforces the composition rule."*

**Step 04 · Simulate the student submission.**
- **WHERE** — **Student submission** panel → **Upload submission** (as student persona, or admin proxy).
- **DO** — Upload any PDF as `Priya's Chapter 1 draft`. Fill the summary field.
- **SEE** — Panel populates with the file, timestamp, "Submitted" pill.
- **SAY** — *"Student's own submission — the workflow now waits on the supervisor's assessment."*

**Step 05 · Supervisor assessment.**
- **WHERE** — **Supervisor assessment** panel → **Start assessment** (as Elena).
- **DO** — Fill: overall rating "Meets expectations", comment "Good progress on the RL survey chapter, methodology needs strengthening", recommend "Pass with minor conditions".
- **SEE** — Assessment saved with timestamp. Milestone status flips to "Under review".
- **SAY** — *"Supervisor's view is on the record before the panel decides — no political pressure to align."*

**Step 06 · Record the panel decision.**
- **WHERE** — Bottom of the milestone page → **Panel decision** button.
- **DO** — Fill: outcome `Progress with conditions`, conditions `Redraft chapter 2 within 12 weeks`, rationale paragraph. Save.
- **SEE** — Decision recorded. Milestone status flips to "Decided". Two things fire: a task to Priya ("chapter 2 redraft due"), a follow-up milestone check scheduled 12 weeks out.
- **SAY** — *"The decision is preserved verbatim. The next-step tasks fire automatically."*

**Step 07 · (Later) Simulate an appeal.**
- **WHERE** — Same milestone → **History** section → **Simulate appeal**.
- **DO** — Click. Fill appeal reason "Panel didn't consider updated methodology plan submitted 2 days before". Save.
- **SEE** — Original decision preserved. New "appeal opened" row in history. Panel state reopens for review.
- **SAY** — *"When a decision is appealed, the panel re-opens. The original stays on the record. Auditors' favourite word: immutable."*

## The line

The state machine does the checking. The paperwork does itself.
