# 22 · AI — Meeting brief · click the button, read the brief, read the evidence

## What it is

Before every supervisor-student meeting, the platform assembles a typed evidence structure — last meeting date, agreed actions, changes since, open flags — and hands it to the AI narrator, which writes a two-sentence brief in colleague voice. Grounding forbids any date or figure not in the evidence; anything the model can't ground falls back to a plain template. Beside the paragraph, three rule-based questions to open the meeting with. **Not shipped by any other PGR system.** In this chapter we open Priya's brief (built from every action we've done in earlier chapters), read it aloud, and cross-check the evidence.

## The clicks

**Step 01 · Open Priya's record.**
- **WHERE** — Sidebar → **Students** → **Priya Nair**.
- **DO** — Look top-right, next to the "Back to students" link.
- **SEE** — Grey secondary button: ✨ **Prepare for meeting**.
- **SAY** — *"Every supervisor walks in cold — "so, where were we?" This is the answer, written before they open the door."*

**Step 02 · Click the button.**
- **WHERE** — The button.
- **DO** — Click. Right-side drawer slides in.
- **SEE** — Reasoning trace with four icons — 🗄️ record → 🧮 compare → ✏️ write → 🎨 lay out.
- **SAY** — *"Opening the record, comparing then and now, writing the brief, laying it out. Every step is real work."*

**Step 03 · Read the brief.**
- **WHERE** — Top of the drawer — tinted card labelled "The brief".
- **DO** — Read the paragraph aloud. Then point at the "What you agreed last time" callout below.
- **SEE** — Paragraph names Priya by first name, addresses "you" the supervisor. Every date and figure appears in the evidence.
- **SAY** — *"The paragraph is the reading. The evidence beside it is the citation. Grounding is a mechanical check — if a number isn't in the evidence, the model can't put it in the prose."*

**Step 04 · Cross-check one figure.**
- **WHERE** — Pick a specific number in the paragraph (e.g. "12 weeks ago").
- **DO** — Point at it. Then find the same figure in the "Changes since" or "Last meeting" evidence beside.
- **SEE** — Same figure — prose left, evidence right.
- **SAY** — *"If I claim a date and it's not in the evidence, the grounded check fails and the paragraph doesn't render. That's the guarantee."*

**Step 05 · Show the openers.**
- **WHERE** — Bottom of the drawer — "Openers you could use".
- **DO** — Point at the three numbered questions.
- **SEE** — Three full-sentence questions, addressed to Priya by first name, one per change or flag.
- **SAY** — *"These are rule-based, not model-authored. "What should I ask?" isn't safe territory to freestyle in — so we don't."*
