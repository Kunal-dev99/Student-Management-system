HESA Student Record — Notification of Changes (SAMPLE for testing, not a real HESA document)

Applies to the 2028/29 collection.

Summary of changes to the Student record specification:

1. A new field SEXORT (Sexual orientation) is introduced, with valid codes 10, 11, 12, 13 and 98.

2. The MODE (mode of study) coding frame gains a new value 99 (Not in attendance).

3. The field TERMTIME (term-time accommodation) is withdrawn from the return.

4. A validation rule is confirmed: ENDDATE must be on or after COMDATE.
